SpeakBook for Mac

Free. Apple silicon Mac, macOS 15 or later. Not on the Mac App Store.

Download SpeakBook for Mac

Install

  1. Open SpeakBook.dmg from your Downloads.
  2. Drag SpeakBook onto the Applications folder beside it.
  3. Open it. It's signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Updates

SpeakBook keeps itself up to date. When a new version is out, it downloads it quietly and installs it the next time your Mac is idle, then reopens. To check yourself, choose SpeakBook › Check for Updates…, or open Settings and find Updates, where you can also turn automatic updates off.

You don't need the Mac app to use SpeakBook. Everything works on your iPhone and iPad, free on the App Store. The Mac app adds extra voices, makes audiobooks, and syncs your books and notes with your other devices.
