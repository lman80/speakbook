Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version: your books, notes and voice notes stay on your own devices. There’s no ads and no tracking. We only receive what you choose to send: feedback, and, if you use the optional SpeakBook Cloud voices, the chapters you ask it to read (deleted once the audio is on your device).

SpeakBook (“SpeakBook: Read Books Aloud” on the App Store) is a reading app for iPhone, iPad and Mac made by Ashton Miller (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What stays on your device

  • Your books and articles, your notes, quotes, bookmarks and reading position, your voice notes and your reading statistics are stored on your devices, in the app and in the SpeakBook folder you can see in the Files app (iPhone and iPad) or in your Documents folder (Mac).
  • Voice notes to text. When you hold the microphone button, the app records a short clip and turns it into text using Apple’s speech recognition. It runs on your device whenever your device supports that. On devices that don’t, Apple may process the audio under Apple’s privacy policy. We never receive your voice notes.
  • Reading aloud on your devices. Apple’s built-in voices, the “Natural” voice and the Mac app’s voices run on your own devices. Book text read with them isn’t sent anywhere.
  • Snap and Scan Pages. Photos you take or pick to read aloud, and scanned pages, are processed on your device with Apple’s text recognition. They aren’t uploaded, and nothing is saved to your photo library.

Syncing between your devices

If you connect your own devices (for example your iPhone and your Mac) in Your Devices, they copy your books, notes, bookmarks, reading position, chosen voices and downloaded audio directly to each other, encrypted, over your local Wi‑Fi or over an address you type in yourself. Devices only pair after you type the code one of them shows into the other. Syncing never goes through our servers or any cloud service, and we can’t see it. If you have SpeakBook Cloud, your paired devices also share its pass so it works on all of them (up to 5).

SpeakBook Cloud (optional, paid)

SpeakBook Cloud makes studio voices without needing a Mac. Only if you use it, we receive:

  • the text of the chapters you ask it to read and the voice you picked;
  • if you picked your own voice: the short recording of your voice and its words, sent with each chapter made in that voice;
  • a random device ID made by the app (so one subscription works on up to 5 devices) and the listening hours you’ve used this billing period;
  • on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s signed record of your subscription (never your card or Apple ID details); on the Mac website version, Stripe handles the payment and has your email and card. We keep only Stripe’s customer and subscription IDs.

Our service runs on Cloudflare (United States and worldwide), and the audio is made on graphics computers we rent from SaladCloud. Chapter text, your voice recording and the audio are deleted as soon as your device has the audio, and in any case within 7 days. The device ID and usage counts are kept while your subscription is active and removed after devices stop being used for 30 days. We never use any of this to train AI models, and never share it except with these providers to run the service.

Your own voice (optional)

You can record a short sample of your own voice so SpeakBook can make a reading voice from it. Only record your own voice, or someone who is with you and agrees. The sample is kept on your devices and copied to your own Mac through device sync. If you use it with SpeakBook Cloud, it’s sent to our service as described above and deleted within 7 days. We don’t use it to identify you. Delete it any time in the app where your voices are listed, and it’s removed from your devices.

Other information that leaves your device

  1. Feedback you choose to send. When you tap the feedback button and press Send, we receive:

    • your message, and any pictures you attach;
    • a screenshot of the screen you were on (it may show text from your book or notes; you can see it and remove it before sending);
    • basic technical details: device model, system and app version, language and time zone, screen size, network type (Wi‑Fi or cellular), memory and free storage, and similar diagnostics;
    • a record of what the app was doing in the minutes before you sent it (for example reading aloud starting and pausing, headphones connecting, downloads and syncing), unless you turn this off in Settings › Help, tour & privacy. It leaves out the words, titles and file names of your books and your notes, but it may include the names of your devices;
    • a random ID created by the app, so we can tell that two messages came from the same person. If you choose to sign in to the feedback panel (optional), your name and email are kept with your reports, votes and tips so you can follow them. You can delete that account in the panel.

    This is sent through a service we run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers, including Cloudflare’s AI features, which may summarise suggestions and match them with existing ideas). It’s stored as an issue in our GitHub repository. Feature ideas may be summarised and shown, without your name or details, in the app’s list of planned ideas that other users can see and vote on. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the app.

    A once-a-day check-in. Once a day the app tells our feedback service which app and system version it’s running and how the feedback button is set up, so we know which versions are still in use. It carries no name, account or identifier.

  2. Purchases. Tips and SpeakBook Cloud on iPhone and iPad are handled by Apple; we receive Apple’s signed receipt so the purchase can be counted and unlocked. On the Mac website version, tips and Cloud are paid through Stripe under Stripe’s privacy policy.

  3. Voice downloads. The first time you choose a downloadable voice, the app downloads its files from a public file host (GitHub or public model hosts). Like any download, the host sees your device’s internet address. No other information is sent.

  4. Things you share yourself.

    • Ask AI: SpeakBook never sends your books to an AI service by itself. The first time you use Ask AI, it asks your permission; after that it copies the passage you chose and opens the AI app or website you pick (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), under that service’s own privacy policy.
    • Sharing a web page to the app: the app downloads that page to save its text for you.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • We don’t sell or rent any information, and we don’t use your books or voice to train AI.
  • No account is needed to use SpeakBook.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log basic request details (such as your internet address) to run the service, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

SpeakBook is suitable for all ages and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Recording a voice to make a reading voice, and buying SpeakBook Cloud, are for people 18 or older (or with a parent’s permission).

Where your information goes, and why

We use the information above only to provide what you asked for (making audio, unlocking purchases, answering feedback). Our providers (Cloudflare, SaladCloud, Stripe, GitHub, Apple) process it for us and may be in the United States or other countries. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal basis is performing our agreement with you, or your consent for your voice recording, which you can withdraw by deleting it.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Everything on your devices is deleted when you delete the app (and its SpeakBook folder).
  • SpeakBook Cloud chapter text and voice recordings: deleted when your device has the audio, at most 7 days.
  • Feedback you sent is kept as long as it’s useful for improving the app. To have it deleted, email us with roughly when you sent it, and we’ll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws such as California’s), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to get a copy, and to have it corrected or deleted. Email us and we’ll help. We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
